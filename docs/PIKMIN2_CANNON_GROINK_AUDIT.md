# Pikmin 2 cannon / groink audit

## Scope and provenance

This is a source audit of the native `pikmin2-research` checkout at revision `632af93787b9c95b63f0c13be32b161375ce3a96` (remote `projectPiki/pikmin2`). Paths below are source-relative to that checkout. No runtime implementation or gameplay test is implied.

The roster IDs are `Rock=19`, `Bomb=36`, `Egg=37`, `Stone=74`, `Kabuto=75`, `MiniHoudai=78`, `Rkabuto=95`, `Fkabuto=96`, and `FminiHoudai=97` (`include/Game/enemyInfo.h:78,95-96,133-134,152-156`). The concrete classes are shared projectile/container classes: `Rock::Obj` serves Rock and Stone (`src/plugProjectNishimuraU/RockMgr.cpp:105-121`); `Kabuto::Obj` is inherited by Green/Kabuto, Red/Rkabuto, and Fix/Fkabuto (`include/Game/Entities/Kabuto.h:378-509`); `MiniHoudai::Obj` is inherited by fixed and roaming groinks (`include/Game/Entities/MiniHoudai.h:441-507`).

## Rock and Stone (19 / 74)

`Rock::Obj::onInit` initializes the projectile as invulnerable, damage-animation/carcass/health-gauge/death-effect disabled, bitter-immune, and with `mIsHoming=false`, `mSourceEnemy=nullptr` (`src/plugProjectNishimuraU/Rock.cpp:47-110`). Falling Rock starts hidden in `ROCK_Wait` or `ROCK_DropWait`; Stone initializes move velocity and starts `ROCK_Move` (`Rock.cpp:47-110`). Wait reveals after a target enters sight (or after the existence-duration delay), Appear scales it up and enables Atari, Fall ends on floor/collision, and Move rolls until health is zero or 15 seconds (`RockState.cpp:30-81,89-137,151-194,203-240`). Dead disables Atari in its execution path and kills after the break animation (`RockState.cpp:261-286`).

Collision is one shared hitbox path. Navi/Pikmin on the floor receive `InteractPress` using the source enemy as attacker when present and general attack damage; Teki receive `InteractAttack` for 250; all non-fake-Pikmin contacts set health to zero, and ordinary rocks suppress their mutual collision event (`Rock.cpp:204-237`). Wall contact also transitions rolling Stone to Dead (`Rock.cpp:240-249`). `ignoreAtari` ignores the source for the first second (`Rock.cpp:298-307`). The collision tree scale is updated while falling/moving (`Rock.cpp:310-341`), so the effective collision volume grows with the visual scale.

Kabuto fires Stone through `createStoneAttack`: it obtains the Rock manager, sets birth type to Stone, spawns at the mouth joint (Y=`25 + mPosition.y`), initializes it, assigns `mSourceEnemy=this`, and enables homing only when `getEnemyTypeID()==Rkabuto` (`src/plugProjectNishimuraU/Kabuto.cpp:268-289`). Homing retargets active Navi in single-player, otherwise nearest Pikmin/Navi using the 180-degree search-angle argument and sight radius; it turns subject to turn-speed/max-angle parameters (`Rock.cpp:368-397`). Non-homing Stone smooths its velocity direction instead (`Rock.cpp:393-402`). Thus Green/Kabuto and buried Fkabuto fire straight Stones; Rkabuto fires homing Stones. The source has no reflection/bounce-back trajectory branch: homing is steering toward a target, not reflection. A failed Rock manager or failed birth is silently tolerated (`Kabuto.cpp:270-289`); native acceptance must cover pool exhaustion and a shooter dying/reusing its manager slot while its Stone remains in flight.

## Kabuto family (75 / 95 / 96)

The shared FSM registers normal wait/turn/move/flick/attack plus buried stay/appear/hide/wait/turn/attack/flick states (`src/plugProjectNishimuraU/KabutoState.cpp:15-31`). Normal attack emits one Stone at animation `KEYEVENT_2`, then returns to wait/turn/flick based on target state (`KabutoState.cpp:347-374`). Buried Fkabuto starts hidden, non-Atari, invulnerable, bitter-immune, and with its life gauge hidden; leaving that state restores Atari, vulnerability, gauge visibility, and model/animation (`KabutoState.cpp:391-438`). Its fixed attack uses the same Stone emission at `KEYEVENT_2` (`KabutoState.cpp:712-750`). Death transitions kill after the death animation; Fkabuto uses its fixed-death animation and ends its wait effect (`KabutoState.cpp:37-59`).

Variant manager allocation is separate but parameter layout is shared: Green, Red, and Fix managers each allocate `Kabuto::Parms` and their own object array (`GreenKabutoMgr.cpp:16-38`, `RedKabutoMgr.cpp:16-38`, `FixKabutoMgr.cpp:16-38`). Green and Fix use the green baby texture; Red uses the red texture (`GreenKabutoMgr.cpp:8-10`, `RedKabutoMgr.cpp:8-10`, `FixKabutoMgr.cpp:8-10`). The PAL-only `setZukanVisible` overrides in FixKabuto are excluded from US behavior (`FixKabuto.cpp:51-74`).

## Bomb (36)

Bomb initializes in `BOMB_Wait`, is non-carcass/non-death-effect, and starts constrained when it is not a drop-group birth (`src/plugProjectMorimuraU/bomb.cpp:67-113`). Capture makes it invulnerable except in versus; release clears the constraint and marks `mHasEscapedCapture` (`bomb.cpp:23-65`). A drop-group bomb bounces or collides with a non-Teki and forces `BOMB_Bomb`; nearby bomb Teki can arm one another with a delay (`bomb.cpp:383-425`). Wait arms on animation start and kills an escaped uncaptured bomb when the counter exceeds 200 (`bombState.cpp:39-102`).

The bomb state ticks damage each frame; after health reaches zero it waits ten ticks, creates the bomb effect, and applies a height-gated spherical blast. Teki receive `mDamageToEnemies`; Navi/Pikmin receive general attack damage with knockback, attributed to the carrier when one exists, then the Bomb is killed (`src/plugProjectMorimuraU/bombState.cpp:103-200`). The blast radius and vertical half-height are parameters (`bombState.cpp:141-188`; `include/Game/Entities/Bomb.h:122-139`). Bomb allocation is a plain object array (`src/plugProjectMorimuraU/bombMgr.cpp:1-34`); the carrier link attributes damage, but durable serialization of that link has not been established.

## Egg (37)

Egg has one wait state. It starts stationary/constrained for non-drop births, and a bounce or non-Teki collision in a drop group makes it health-zero and exposes its life gauge (`src/plugProjectMorimuraU/egg.cpp:22-67,169-191`). Wait breaks it when health is zero, calls `genItem`, creates down/bomb effects, plays the break sound, and kills it (`src/plugProjectMorimuraU/eggState.cpp:38-77`).

`genItem` chooses from one/five pellets, single/double nectar, ten Mitites, spicy spray, or bitter spray using the five chance parameters; forced drop type overrides the random choice. Spicy/bitter are downgraded to nectar until the corresponding first-spray demo flag exists (`src/plugProjectMorimuraU/egg.cpp:243-329`). Mitite-group allocation failure falls back to nectar (`egg.cpp:326-346`); each item birth is null-checked, so acceptance must include exhausted pellet, honey, and Mitite pools. Drop velocity is generally upward 250; double nectar splits horizontally; Mitites use upward 200 (`egg.cpp:243-385`).

## MiniHoudai / Groink (78 / 97)

The FSM has Dead, Rebirth, Lost, Attack, Flick, Turn, path/home walking states (`src/plugProjectNishimuraU/MiniHoudaiState.cpp:18-32`). Damage applies full damage when a collision part is supplied and quarter damage otherwise (`MiniHoudai.cpp:162-171`). Attack event 2 pauses motion and starts rotation/charge; event 3 ends charge and emits smoke; event 4 emits shells under the exact guard `!isFinishMotion() || !(mHealth <= 0)`; event 5 pauses and finishes rotation (`MiniHoudaiState.cpp:281-340`). Fixed ID 97 enables `EB_Constrained` during init; both variants reset waypoint/shotgun state and start TurnPath (`MiniHoudai.cpp:36-55`); the fixed pedestal variant shares the base manager/parameters and has its own manager/object allocation (`FixMiniHoudaiMgr.cpp:10-40`). PAL-only Zukan accounting in FixMiniHoudai is not US behavior (`FixMiniHoudai.cpp:15-39`).

Shotgun emission draws up to three inactive nodes, spawns each at 25 units ahead of the head joint, adds random directional spread, uses `mShellSpeed`, and marks only the first shell for camera/rumble feedback (`src/plugProjectNishimuraU/MiniHoudaiShotGun.cpp:1345-1384`). Each shell is a moving sphere of radius 10 traced through the map, with gravity `velocity.y -= 20`; floor/wall contact fades its effect and returns it to the inactive pool. It also returns to the pool when any absolute position delta from its owner exceeds 1000 (`MiniHoudaiShotGun.cpp:86-149`). The swept segment applies `InteractBomb` to Navi/Pikmin with owner attack damage and directional knockback, applies `InteractBomb` for 100 to Teki, and performs endpoint wind/bomb checks (`MiniHoudaiShotGun.cpp:159-243`). Active nodes are reclaimed by `doUpdateCommon` (`MiniHoudaiShotGun.cpp:1750-1762`), and force-finished on teardown (`MiniHoudaiShotGun.cpp:1768-1779`).

When a groink carcass pellet remains alive, its gauge delay elapses, then health regenerates at `maxHealth/respawnRate`. At full health the pellet is killed and a *new* same-type object is born at the old position/orientation/duration/Piklopedia flag; the old object enters `MINIHOUDAI_Rebirth` (`src/plugProjectNishimuraU/MiniHoudai.cpp:293-318`). This is replacement-object revival, not same-object resurrection. If the pellet has died, the gauge is inactivated (`MiniHoudai.cpp:320-325`). Dead animation itself kills the object after its key events (`MiniHoudaiState.cpp:38-70`). Drops and corpse creation also pass through shared `EnemyBase::onKill` (`src/plugProjectYamashitaU/enemyBase.cpp:1289-1438`); family-local absence is not evidence of no drops.

## Reset, persistence, and unknowns

Creature save has an empty default `doSave`; `Creature::save` optionally writes position and calls it (`include/Game/Creature.h:254`, `src/plugProjectKandoU/creature.cpp:262-275`). Generator save tracks creature position only when the reserved flag is set (`src/plugProjectKandoU/gameGenerator.cpp:282-316`), while enemy-manager birth supplies existence duration and drop/treasure/pellet metadata (`src/plugProjectYamashitaU/enemyMgrBase.cpp:213-236`). No family override that serializes Bomb, Egg, Rock/Stone, Kabuto, or MiniHoudai transient state was found. Therefore save/resume behavior for armed bombs, in-flight Stones/shells, Kabuto buried/attack state, and groink carcass regeneration remains unresolved; do not infer reset or persistence across every cave/day transition.

## Native acceptance tests

1. Exhaust each manager pool while triggering Kabuto Stone attack, Egg drops, and three-shell Groink attack; verify null births do not crash, leave stale ownership pointers, or prevent later normal births.
2. Spawn Rock and Stone against floor, wall, water, Navi, Pikmin, Teki, and another Rock. Verify scale-grown hitbox, 250 Teki impact, source attribution, mutual-rock suppression, wall death, water effects, and 15-second timeout.
3. Compare Kabuto/Rkabuto/Fkabuto: confirm only Rkabuto curves Stone toward a valid target; confirm Fkabuto hides/non-Atari state, appears, attacks, and dies with fixed animations.
4. Arm Bomb through bounce, collision, Teki chain, capture/release, and bitter hits. Verify escaped timeout at counter >200, ten-tick detonation delay, radius plus vertical gate, carrier attribution, and pool exhaustion.
5. Break Egg repeatedly with forced drop types and each chance boundary; verify first-spray gating, double nectar, Mitite fallback, and null item births.
6. Damage roaming and fixed Groinks with/without collision parts; observe full versus quarter damage, death animation, gauge delay, replacement-object Rebirth, and replacement allocation failure. Confirm whether the replacement inherits position, direction, duration, and Piklopedia state exactly.
7. Save/resume at each transient state (armed Bomb, falling Rock, fired Stone/shell, buried Kabuto, and Groink carcass) and across cave/day transitions. Record native results because source serialization coverage is incomplete.
