## Source and lifetime contract

An actual source Onion root carries the selected session fingerprint, immutable
source SHA/key/UID/species, and the incarnation assigned by successful native
birth. An emitted member additionally retains its exact corpse or numeric-child
cause, per-session serial, and seed ordinal. Authored stock retains its complete
canonical `OriginalPikiBody` origin. Neither stock counts nor a native pointer
can manufacture either family of origins.

Reward admission precedes corpse receipt consumption and pending-count changes.
Actual head allocation binds the already admitted member; a genuine null head
allocation stores that same member as one Leaf. Pluck/swallow transfers the
member only after body allocation and initialization succeed, before head kill.
Deposit preserves current maturity and wild history. Withdrawal selects the
highest maturity, then FIFO within that maturity; it changes lineage only after
successful body allocation. Unknown or mismatched stock refuses before queue
reservation, leg RNG, or allocation. FIFO is a deliberate policy, not a proved
retail order within one maturity.

The common body reader is a process-lifetime singleton installed before labels
exist. Ownership survives a failed admission query, preventing ordinary fallback.
Kill/pool reuse retires the actual associated lifetime. The post-Party scene
callback retires BODY pointers while retaining immutable history. Live HEADs or
pending rewards still refuse course unload; authenticated physical restoration
is not implemented here.

## Short human gameplay script

Use an authenticated original campaign and its actual admitted source catalog,
with 20 starting Pikmin and the centered 960×540 window. Keep saves and logs in
the private ignored output directory. Record the selected session/catalog and
native executable hash before starting.

1. Reach and boot the actual source Red Onion through ordinary gameplay. Confirm
   its source UID/key and birth incarnation in the log. Recruit the starting
   source Pikmin through the actual whistle/contact mechanic.
2. Defeat a source Chappy in combat, attach at least ten actual carriers, and let
   them deliver its retained body. Confirm one consumed corpse receipt and one
   twelve-member pending range; killing or merely attaching carriers must not
   grant population.
3. Let the Onion emit its sprouts. Pluck one after its maturity changes, then
   deposit and withdraw that Pikmin. Check that member serial, birth root, full
   reward cause, and seed ordinal remain identical and maturity is preserved.
4. Fill the field through actual gameplay, deliver another distinct body, and
   verify each allocation refused by the field/pool becomes exactly one stored
   Leaf. Free a field slot and withdraw it; no second reward or lost member is
   permitted. A failed allocation must preserve the pending exit and member.
5. Attempt repeated delivery observation and recycled pool slots. Each source
   receipt pays once; a recycled pointer has a fresh lifetime. Count-only
   inherited stock stays unavailable instead of acquiring invented ancestry.

Do not run save/resume or advertise campaign completion until the SAVE owner
publishes authenticated HEAD/BODY/pending/stock restoration for this family.
